  - the town's story, its surroundings and population, "as a basis for identifying belonging groups"
  - landforms in the town (mountains, hills, plains, streams)

## X6. Causal exploration (Gopnik / Schulz / Legare)
- **Finding (M):**
  - The blicket detector (Gopnik & Sobel 2000) was used with ages 2-4. Children aged 4.5 (and to a lesser extent 3.5) make screening-off and backward-blocking inferences (Sobel, Tenenbaum & Gopnik 2004).
  - **Schulz & Bonawitz 2007:** preschoolers tell confounded from unconfounded evidence and explore more after confounded evidence, while unconfounded evidence leaves the usual novelty preference.
  - **Legare 2012** (secondary): evidence that conflicts with prior belief drives explanations, and the kind of explanation predicts later exploration.
  - Critique: the blicket results are indirect, and 3-year-olds did not show backward blocking in one study.
- Sources: [Schulz & Bonawitz preprint](https://cocosci.dreamhosters.com/Liz/SchulzBonawitzInPress.pdf); [Bonawitz, Lim & Schulz 2007](https://cocosci.dreamhosters.com/Liz/BonawitzLimSchulz2007.pdf); [Bonawitz et al. 2012](https://cocosci.princeton.edu/Liz/BonawitzetalBalance12.pdf); [UvA chapter](https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/1539332/104985_08.pdf); [metacognitive awareness of confounded evidence](https://cacs-dev.squarespace.com/s/young-childrens-metacognitive-awareness-of-confounded-evidence.pdf)
- **Change to the spec:** the "Mystery Machine" mechanic (S.INQ.02) deliberately includes **confounded trials** (two objects on together), followed by "רוצה לבדוק שוב?". After a surprise, the child is asked to "explain" ("למה לדעתך?") before testing again. No claims of general-intelligence gains (grade B, basic research, not an intervention).

## X7. Background knowledge → comprehension (why packs matter)
- **Finding (M-H):** content-literacy RCTs from the MORE project (Kim et al.):
  - **Longitudinal RCT, grades 1→2:** thematic science and social-studies lessons plus summer reading of related informational texts. Gains on general reading comprehension and on science reading, with a transfer **ES = .18**.
  - **Cluster RCT** (Kim, Relyea, Burkhauser, Scherer & Rich 2021, *Ed Psych Review*): science vocabulary depth **ES = 0.30**, reading comprehension **ES = 0.11**, argumentative writing **ES = 0.24**.
  - Also: background knowledge matters more for informational text than for narrative (a review of 23 studies, ages 6-12, via Wexler).
  - Recht & Leslie 1988 (H, earlier note).
- Sources: [Harvard Usable Knowledge](https://www.gse.harvard.edu/ideas/usable-knowledge/23/03/building-background-knowledge-science-improves-reading-comprehension); [Wexler, evidence](https://nataliewexler.substack.com/p/yes-there-is-evidence-that-building); [aminer: longitudinal RCT](https://aminer.org/pub/62d16fad5aee126c0fd869e8/a-longitudinal-randomized-trial-of-a-sustained-content-literacy-intervention-from-first); [Ed Psych Review 2021](https://springerlink.bibliotecabuap.elogim.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10648-021-09609-6.pdf)
- **Change to the spec:** (1) packs are built as **thematic text sets** (3-4 connected packs per series), not as isolated "fun facts". (2) Every pack has 5-7 target words, each repeated at least 4 times across the text, the game and the quiz. (3) Expected effects are small (≈0.1-0.3 SD). This is written into the spec as an honest expectation.

## X8. Computational thinking for ages 3-8: evidence and progressions
- **Finding (M-H):**
  - From the earlier note (H): ages 3-8 meta-analysis d = 0.83 (17 studies); unplugged CT g = 0.63-1.03; ScratchJr K-2 over 7 weeks, all mastered the basics, with differences by grade.
  - **ScratchJr "Animated Genres"** (DevTech): 3 modules (interactive collage → story → game), 2 units each, 6 weeks × 2 lessons a week. Module 1 runs: instructions and sequencing → same-block sequencing and motion → green flag and end block. Kindergarten and grade 1 work as a whole class; grade 2 works in small groups.
  - **CAL (Coding as Another Language):** ScratchJr K-2. The grade-2 version has 24 lessons, 18 hours, plugged and unplugged. *ECRQ* 64 (2023) 394-404 (Bers, Blake-West, Kapoor, Levinson, Relkin, Unahalekhaka). **Results not retrieved.**
  - **KIBO:** screenless, wooden blocks, pre-K to grade 2. An 8-week pilot showed mastery of basic concepts in pre-K.
  - Background: Bers' "Seven Powerful Ideas" (algorithms, modularity, control structures, representation, hardware/software, design process, debugging). TechCheck is an unplugged K-2 CT assessment (Relkin & Bers).
- Sources: [ScratchJr Animated Genres PDF](https://www.scratchjr.org/curricula/animatedgenres/full.pdf); [ScratchJr CAL](https://scratchfoundation.org/learn/learning-library/scratchjr-cal); [CAL site](https://sites.bc.edu/codingasanotherlanguage/?p=596); [ECRQ 2023 CAL PDF](https://sites.bc.edu/devtech/wp-content/uploads/sites/181/2023/05/1-s2.0-S0885200623000571-main.pdf); [NSF: teaching tykes to program robots](https://www.nsf.gov/news/teaching-tykes-program-robots); [Bers 2019 CAL article](https://sites.bc.edu/devtech/wp-content/uploads/sites/181/2019/11/Bers2019_Article_CodingAsAnotherLanguageAPedago.pdf)
- **Change to the spec:** the C strand is built on the Seven Powerful Ideas. The Code Studio progression copies Animated Genres (collage → story → game). There is an unplugged layer for every concept, and a TechCheck-style probe every 8 weeks for K1/K2 (untimed, unrewarded).

## X10. Executive function training: transfer
- **Finding (M):**
  - Kassai et al. 2019 (*Psych Bulletin*): EF training in children shows **no convincing far transfer** between EF components (g = 0.11, k = 17, ns).
  - A preschool meta-analysis (ages 3-6) found an overall effect g = 0.35 (k = 123) on EF measures.
  - Melby-Lervåg & Hulme 2013 (secondary): working-memory training improves working memory but does not transfer.
  - Diamond & Ling 2016 (secondary): activities embedded in daily life are preferable.
- Sources: [Kassai 2019 PDF](https://www.gwern.net/doc/dual-n-back/2019-kassai.pdf); [Frontiers 2019 preschool EF](https://www.frontiersin.org/article/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02812/full); [PMC6965160](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6965160); [Edinburgh near/far transfer meta](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/a-meta-analysis-of-the-experimental-evidence-on-the-near-and-far-/)
- **Change to the spec:** L.EF.* games appear as **play and movement** (Freeze Dance, Day-Night, HTKS) and as **internal calibration** (EFCalibration, from the interest note). There are no "brain training" claims, no EF scores shown to parent or child, and no EF mastery gate.

## X11. Financial literacy for ages 5-9
- **Finding (M):**
  - The 2021 National Standards for Personal Financial Education (CEE + Jump$tart) have **six topics**: Earning Income, Spending, Saving, Investing, Managing Credit, Managing Risk. Benchmarks are set at the end of grades 4, 8 and 12. There is **no separate national K-2 band**.
  - Grade 4 examples (Kansas crosswalk): 4-1 job options are linked to a person's abilities and interests; 4-2 building skills raises earning potential.
  - States (Washington, draft) have K-2 standards in Earning Income, Spending and Saving, with Credit and Managing Risk added in grade 2.
  - Whitebread & Bingham 2013 (MAS, Cambridge; **secondary only**): money habits are "taking shape by around age 7". The "set by age 7" headline is overstated.
  - Background (L): children under about 7-8 do not reliably recognize the persuasive intent of ads (APA Task Force 2004).
  - Earlier note: there is no evidence on teaching financial literacy through an in-game economy.
- Sources: [2021 National Standards PDF (KSDE)](https://www.ksde.gov/Portals/0/CSAS/CSAS%20Home/Financial%20Literacy/2021%20National%20Standards%20for%20Personal%20Financial%20Education.pdf); [KSDE summary](https://www.ksde.gov/Portals/0/CSAS/CSAS%20Home/Financial%20Literacy/Summary%20of%20National%20Standards%20for%20Personal%20Financial%20Education.pdf); [KSDE crosswalk](https://www.ksde.gov/Portals/0/CSAS/CSAS%20Home/Financial%20Literacy/Financial%20Literacy%20Crosswalk.pdf); [WA draft standards](https://ospi.k12.wa.us/sites/default/files/2025-11/draft-wa-learning-standards-financial-education.docx); [Yahoo on MAS 2013](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/blogs/pay-day-/kids-develop-money-habits-age-seven-study-200000613.html)
- **Change to the spec:** F uses 4 of the 6 topics for G/S (earning, spending, saving, risk) plus giving and ad literacy. Investing and credit get only a "savings tree" simulation for K1. **No interest on the in-game wallet** (D1: coins come from mastery and effort, not time). F claims are graded C.

## X13. ScratchJr in Hebrew / RTL
- **Finding (L):** no source confirms Hebrew or RTL support in ScratchJr. Sphero Edu (Scratch-based) mirrors the block canvas only when the device is in Hebrew or Arabic. Scratch 3 has documented RTL bugs.
- Sources: [Sphero Edu Scratch RTL](https://medium.com/sphero-sprk/sphero-edu-implements-scratch-e8242e0b0deb); [scratch-gui RTL issue mirror](https://vat.pages.dev/scratchfoundation/scratch-gui/issues/10067)
- **Change to the spec (design decision, ADR-06-01):** we do not depend on ScratchJr. We build our own "Code Studio" on Skia (E2) and pick a direction convention:
  - G band: a **horizontal code strip running right to left** (the Hebrew reading direction), with a start flag on the right.
  - S band: a **vertical stack running top to bottom** (direction-neutral, a bridge to Scratch).
  - Movement arrows always mean **absolute screen directions** (⬅ = left of the screen) and never "forward in reading direction".

## X14. Spatial skills, physical activity (background, not searched this round)
- (L, background, well known) Uttal et al. 2013 (*Psych Bulletin*) meta-analysis: spatial skills are trainable (g ≈ 0.47), with durability and transfer within the spatial domain. Spatial ability correlates with math.
- (L, background) WHO guidelines 2019/2020:
  - ages 3-4: ≥180 min of activity a day, ≥60 of it moderate to vigorous; sedentary screen time ≤1 hour
  - ages 5-17: an average of ≥60 min of moderate to vigorous activity a day
- **Change to the spec:** L.SPAT is part of the core of L (the strongest evidence in the strand, grade B+). H.MOVE.01 adds 30-60 s movement breaks per episode and offline movement missions. The app does not replace activity; it hands off to it.
